Claim help

A claim stuck in queries or rejected? Bring us the file.

Health claims are where people most need someone on their side. We know the paperwork the insurer and the TPA ask for, and we follow up until there's a decision.

  • Cashless approvals at network hospitals: we coordinate with the TPA desk.
  • Reimbursement files checked for missing bills, reports and discharge papers before they go in.
  • Queries and rejections reviewed against your policy wording, with a reply or appeal prepared.
  • Motor claims: intimation, surveyor visit and garage follow-up.

Claim decisions are made by the insurer. We help you present the claim properly and keep it moving.
