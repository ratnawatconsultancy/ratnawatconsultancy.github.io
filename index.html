Common questions

What do I need to renew my policy?

Your old policy copy or policy number, and your mobile number. For a vehicle, keep the RC handy. Tell us if your address, vehicle or nominee has changed.

How do renewal reminders work?

We send a WhatsApp reminder 30, 15, 7 and 1 day before a policy ends or a premium falls due. Please tell us if your mobile number changes.

What if I have missed my renewal date?

Call us as soon as you can. What can be done depends on the type of policy and the insurer, so please don't wait.

What documents are needed for a health claim?

Usually the claim form, policy copy or health card, hospital bills and receipts, discharge summary, doctor's prescriptions, test reports, ID proof and bank details. The insurer or TPA may ask for more.

What documents are needed for a motor claim?

Usually the claim form, policy copy, RC, driving licence, photos of the damage and the garage estimate. A police report may be needed for theft or a serious accident. Tell us as soon as it happens.

What documents are needed for an LIC claim?

It depends on the claim. For a death claim, the family usually needs the policy document, death certificate, claim form and the nominee's ID and bank details. LIC may ask for more, and we will guide you through it.

Will my claim be approved?

The insurer decides every claim, so nobody can promise the result. We help you prepare the file properly and follow up with the insurer.
